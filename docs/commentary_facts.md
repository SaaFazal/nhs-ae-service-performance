## Frame (3 short paragraphs, use your own words)

**1. What changed.** One or two sentences: the headline 4 hour %, the change from last month, and the gap to the target. Then the 12 hour waits.

**2. Why (what the data suggests).** Demand went up: attendances rose 4.3% in a month and are higher than last September. Pressure showed in admissions, with 12 hour waits up 5,631. The fall happened in almost every region, so it looks system wide rather than one trust. Last September showed the same dip, so part of it is seasonal. Only say what the data shows. Do not claim causes the data cannot prove (staffing, strikes and so on).

**3. What to look at.** Two or three actions or watch points, for example: the North West (biggest regional fall, and 4 of the worst 10 trusts, including East Cheshire at the bottom); Birmingham Women's and Children's (a 15.5 pt drop in one month is unusual, so check whether it is a data issue or a real problem); and the winter outlook (if September is already worse than last year, winter will be harder, and the March 2027 target of 82% needs about 7.6 pts of improvement).

## Rules

* No numbers that are not in the table above.
* Plain English, short sentences. A manager should be able to read it in one minute.
